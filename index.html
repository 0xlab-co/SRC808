⚙️ Driver Extension

"SRC808" would like to use a new driver extension

You can enable this extension in Login Items & Extensions. Driver extensions run in the background and can monitor hardware accessories connected to your Mac.

Purpose: SRC808 uses a driver extension to properly route and control the volume of individual applications on a system level without compromising your Mac's global audio output.

"SRC808.app" wants access to control "Spotify.app"

Allowing control will provide access to documents and data in "Spotify.app", and to perform actions within that app.

Purpose: SRC808 uses Apple Events to safely read now-playing track info (title, artist, album art) from Spotify and Apple Music to display on the Mini Media Controller. We do not modify or access your personal data.

Input Monitoring / Accessibility

SRC808 intercepts volume keys to show its own HUD. This is managed automatically.

Purpose: To replace the default macOS volume overlay with SRC808's enhanced UI when you press the media keys on your keyboard, we need permission to listen for those specific key presses.
